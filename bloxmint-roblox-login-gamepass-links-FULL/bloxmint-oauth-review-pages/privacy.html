BBloxMint Gamepass Store

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the BloxMint Gamepass Store ("BloxMint", "we") handles information when you connect a Roblox account, browse gamepasses, and use verification features.

Information we use

  • Basic Roblox account information provided through your authorization, such as Roblox user ID, username, display name, and profile image.
  • Gamepass identifiers, gamepass ownership-check results, and records used to avoid duplicate donation announcements.
  • Technical information necessary to operate the service, including login sessions and essential cookies.

We do not ask for or collect your Roblox password. Roblox authentication is handled by Roblox.

How we use it

  • To link your Roblox account to your session on the BloxMint website.
  • To let you browse gamepasses and verify ownership, including checks for eligible role passes.
  • To record verifications and help prevent repeated announcements for the same pass.
  • To maintain site security and investigate errors.

Discord announcements

When a donation verification is successfully recorded, BloxMint may send a message in its Discord server showing your Roblox username or display name, the gamepass name and listed price, and a link or image associated with your Roblox profile. This information may be visible to other members of that Discord server.

Services involved

Roblox processes account sign-in and gamepass purchases under Roblox's own policies. Cloudflare hosts the website and database. Discord receives verification announcements. We share only the information needed for the described features.

Storage and retention

Account-linking session data and verification records are stored to operate the website and prevent repeated verification announcements. We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, security, and handling requests. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of your information, subject to records that must be kept for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can choose not to link your Roblox account. You may revoke BloxMint's Roblox app authorization through Roblox account settings. Revoking access may prevent future account-linked features, but may not immediately remove previously stored verification records.

Contact and data requests

For questions, privacy requests, or data deletion requests, contact the BloxMint team through our Discord server by reaching a server administrator.
